About Us

Who We Are

Naledi Bakery is an individually owned bakery, supported by our team we are dedicated to creating fresh, handmade treats using quality ingreadients, including love as well as traditional baking techniques.Our goal is serve our community solely with quality products while creating a warm and welcoming experience for everyone who visits us.

Our Location

Naledi Bakeries can be found deep within the heart of Daveyton township.Where we serve Daveyton residents and surrounding areas with passion and commitment. If you wish to visit us and experience the warmth yourself visit 3345 Mocke street, Daveyton on your trusted GPS and you will be right at our doorstep.
